Build the roads. Watch the traffic.

TrafficSim is a road builder and traffic simulator that runs in your browser. Lay out a town, zone homes and shops, and every car finds its own way through what you built. It's free, with nothing to install.

Roads the way you'd draw them

Straight roads, smooth curves, one-way streets, medians, up to three lanes each way, and bridges and tunnels where roads cross. Snap guides keep it tidy.

Junctions that behave

Traffic signals, stop and give-way signs, and roundabouts. With the lane connector you choose exactly which lane goes where.

Drivers with somewhere to be

Homes send cars to shops and offices. Each driver plans the fastest route, picks a lane, merges, queues and reroutes around jams.

A town that lives through the day

The clock runs with the simulation. Shadows move across the day, windows and street lights come on at night, and you can bring in cloud, rain or wind. Homes and businesses grow as cars come and go.

Your first junction takes a minute

A short tutorial walks you through it. Your map saves in the browser as you build, so you can pick up where you left off.
