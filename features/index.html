Features

TrafficSim is a small, detailed world you build road by road. Here's what you can do in it today.

Building roads

  • Straight roads, curves and complex curves, or keep clicking to draw one long road that bends smoothly.
  • One to three lanes each way, one-way streets, medians, footpaths and street trees.
  • Speed limits from 30 to 110 km/h on each road. Drivers prefer faster routes.
  • Bridges, tunnels and ramps between heights, with junctions in the air.
  • Snap to road ends, the grid, guidelines and 45° angles. A readout shows length and angle as you draw.
  • Build a parallel copy of a road as you draw it, and bulldoze one road at a time or a whole area.

Junctions

Signals
Phases with green and amber timings you set, and turn on red where you allow it.
Stop and give way
All-way stops served first come, first served, or priority roads with signs on the side roads.
Roundabouts
Raised islands. Cars entering wait for a gap in the ring traffic.
Lane connector
Choose which lanes in lead to which lanes out. The painted arrows follow.

Homes, shops and offices

  • Zoning tiles appear beside every road. Pick a building and place it, facing the road.
  • Villas and apartments send cars out. Shops and offices are where they go.
  • Choose where each home's cars head: any business, or only the ones you pick.
  • Buildings level up as cars come and go, with scaffolding while they grow.
  • Driveways, car parks and parking bays where cars actually park.

Drivers

Every car is simulated on its own, 60 times a second. Each one plans the fastest route and drives it the way people do.

  • Plan lanes several roads ahead and avoid queued lanes.
  • Change lanes where there's a gap, and get pushier near their turn.
  • Merge in turn where a lane ends.
  • Don't block the box or stop across driveways.
  • Reroute when held up, and stay home when the roads are jammed.
  • Click any car to see its route as a blue line.

Traffic rules you can tune

Each map has its own rules in Settings, and changes apply live. Start from a preset (Chill, Standard, Hurry or Mad Max), then adjust right of way, signal timings, the gaps drivers accept at roundabouts and driveways, lane change reaction times and more. You can also switch between left- and right-hand traffic.

A stats panel shows the results: trips finished, mean trip time, lane changes, reroutes and cars stuck.

The world around it

  • A clock that runs with the simulation, with moving shadows, street lights and lit windows at night.
  • Sunny, cloudy, rainy or windy weather.
  • Trees and bushes along the roads, saved with your map.
  • Internet radio from Radio Paradise while you build.
  • A free camera: pan, turn, zoom and tilt from top-down to a low 3D view.

Your maps

Your map saves in the browser as you build and is there on your next visit. You can also save maps as files and open them again later or on another computer.
