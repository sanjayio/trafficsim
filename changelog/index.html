Changelog

What's new in TrafficSim. For every change, see the commit history on GitHub.

27 November 2026

Bridges, tunnels and a new look

  • Bridges and tunnels at set step heights, with ramps between heights and junctions in the air.
  • Tunnel walls around junctions, edge lines, and ramp cuttings that end at the tunnel mouth.
  • A Buildings tool, with new apartment, shop and office models and new cars.
  • Scaffolding goes up when a building levels up.
  • Raised medians and roundabout islands with kerbs.
  • Unbroken kerbs past buildings and dropped kerbs at driveways.
  • Esc closes menus. Big cities run faster.
26 November 2026

Traffic rules, smarter drivers

  • Traffic rules for each map, editable in Settings and applied live.
  • Speed limits on each road. Routes weigh both speed and turns.
  • Drivers plan their lanes ahead, change lanes where there's a gap, merge in turn and reroute when stuck.
  • Turn on red at signalled junctions.
  • Gridlock handling: drivers don't block the box, keep driveways clear, and stay home when the roads are jammed.
  • A traffic stats panel, opened from the fps readout. Click a car to see its route.
  • A new look: villa variants, trees, zebra crossings, raised footpaths and kerbs, and road textures.
  • Trees and bushes along roads, saved with the map.
  • The clock runs with the simulation. Weather, tool, speed and radio station are remembered.
  • Road and traffic options are laid out in titled rows. The game starts paused, with the Select tool.
  • A short animation on each tutorial slide.
25 November 2026

First release

  • Road building with straight and curved roads, snapping and guides.
  • Traffic: homes and businesses, cars that find their route, junction rules and the lane connector.
  • Building types on zoning tiles that grow over time, with car parks, driveways and slip roads.
  • Sky, weather, time of day, shadows and night lights.
  • Street trees along footpaths.
  • Your map is kept in the browser between visits, and a New map dialog with a tutorial helps you start.
  • Internet radio, and a Support button.
